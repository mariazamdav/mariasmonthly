what i did this month

1. Worked out a lot. I am almost as strong as I was in 2023! It feels amazing. It also means I'm eating a crazy amount of food, and that's bizarre. I told my grandma I'm "eating like a man" and I think that triggered her, hahahaha. I'm also really annoying about having literally all my gym accessories be BRIGHT pink because that's my instinct when I'm in super masculine spaces.

2. Tried rope climbing (fun!) and quit climbing. I don't like it when I go by myself. It's scary and it's really expensive. I tried workout classes, which occasionally I enjoy but, idk guys, nothing hits like weightlifting. I think the solution might be keep the morning workouts (lifting and running) and replace the rest for friends and creative time.

3. Went to a Broadway in Boston show: Suffs, about the US suffragists. Very cool!

4. Went to my first NBA game, Celtics vs. Timberwolves. I got to see Jason Tatum and Jalen Brown irl and may have developed a crush on both of them. I audibly gasped at Brown's muscles in the jumbotron.

5. Went to coffeeshop day at my friend's house (so cool!) and convinced someone to ditch buying a new phone for an Apple Watch. They opened up my eyes to how my watch can further separate me from my phone. I am now tempted to buy a watch with cellular so I can leave my phone at home.

6. Wrote my first two Substack articles. Please, read them? They're special to me and I'm really excited to develop a new hobby!

7. I got a Massachusetts ID, which means… LATIN CLUB ERA COMING SOON!! CATCH ME ON THE DANCEFLOOR.

8. I'm finding a niche in my research that I'm genuinely excited about. Yayyyy. I've started to use my assignments to go deeper into the AI systems being implemented (or failing to be so) in Latin American bureaucracies. There's interesting stuff happening, loaded with lots of conflicting interests and power dynamics.

9. Venezuela became BASEBALL WORLD CHAMPION. We're holding that title for the next 4 years. You really can't tell me anything now. We're so back.

10. Paid taxes! Renewed my lease! Bought a new pan to cook easier! Did all the adult things adults do!

11. Turned 25 :)
