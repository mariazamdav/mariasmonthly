01

recap of my month

1. Went to the club, as promised, and had an amazing time. Latin clubs remain PEAK.

2. Went to a women's hockey game, Boston vs. Montreal! I had the best rum and coke of my life.

3. Did my first and second PhD research interviews. I'm so excited to embed myself in useful and interesting networks.

4. Watched The Drama. I hadn't found a movie so engaging and creative in a long time. I'm still not exactly sure what to do with the moral aftermess it leaves.

5. Gave my first research presentations and got feedback from my peers. It was nice and cozy, and I felt validated. I really can't wait to do research full time.

6. Figured out the hunger situation. I am no longer hungry. In fact, I somehow tilted the scale toward feeling too full all the time. I'm dialing back again.

7. Figured out I was going too hard at the gym because my form was suffering and I needed super long rests. I left my ego behind and lowered the weight for a few weeks. I have started to up it bit by bit.

8. From the Apple Watch research, I changed mobile companies to a cheaper one, but I decided against buying an Apple Watch to get away from my phone. I went back to my phone and scrolling reels and I decided, again, that I don't like it.
